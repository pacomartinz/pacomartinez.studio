About

I'm Paco Martínez, a director and filmmaker based in Andalusia. My work has moved across cinema, fashion, lifestyle and branded projects, always with an eye for atmosphere, movement and a strong sense of place.

Tennis brings many of those things together for me. Not just what happens on court, but the clubs, hotels, destinations and people around it — the details and experiences that give the game a world of its own.
